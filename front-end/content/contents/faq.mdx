# Frequently asked questions

1. **If you use our website to convert your data, which studies do you have to cite?**
    
    
    You have to cite the following: 
    
    1- The study about **TreatMeta** website 
    
    2- The study about the used conversion(s) 
    
    
    *For example,* if you used our website to combine multiple means and standard deviations, cite the following:
    
    1- The study about **TreatMeta** website
    
    2- Cochrane Handbook: “Higgins JPT, Thomas J, Chandler J, Cumpston M, Li T, Page MJ, Welch VA (editors). *Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions* version 6.4 (updated August 2023). Cochrane, 2023. Available from www.training.cochrane.org/handbook.”
    
    N.B. Download the needed citations from the provided list in **“Cite Us page”.**
